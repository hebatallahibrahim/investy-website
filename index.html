faq

Questions we hear before every project

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly — we reply quickly.

Why are you called Investy?

Because we treat every project as an investment. We put serious time, effort and expertise into your product up front, and we measure our success by the return it brings you: revenue, users and time saved. That's also why we stay after launch to keep growing it.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, platforms and timeline. After a free strategy call we send a clear proposal with a fixed scope and price, so there are no surprises. For ongoing scale and growth work we also offer monthly partnerships.

How long does it take to launch?

A focused website or MVP typically takes weeks, not months. Larger platforms and apps are delivered in phases, so you can launch a first version early and keep improving it. You'll get a realistic timeline in the proposal.

How do you use AI?

In two ways. We build AI features and automations for clients, from assistants to smart search and forecasting. We also use AI tools in our own process to research, prototype and test faster, with experienced engineers reviewing everything before it ships.

We're outside Egypt. Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. We already work with clients in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the USA and the Netherlands. We agree on overlapping hours, hold regular video check-ins and keep everything visible in shared tools, so distance never slows the project down.

Who owns the code and design?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the source code, designs and accounts are yours, and we hand everything over with documentation.

What happens after launch?

This is where we're different. We can stay on to maintain, secure and improve the product, and run growth work — marketing, conversion optimisation and analytics — so your investment keeps paying back.

Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We start with a technical and UX review, tell you honestly what's working and what isn't, and agree a plan to stabilise, improve and grow it.
